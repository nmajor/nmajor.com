## Relevant source text

In 2024, Rockwell said its Singapore manufacturing location had made significant progress in its Connected Enterprise programme. It listed autonomous mobile robots, lights-off warehouse automation, advanced process automation, robotic arms, and real-time asset-performance monitoring.

The report also said: "The team has also activated AI-guided troubleshooting assistance, predictive maintenance, and process optimization to enhance work and workforce retention."

Rockwell said it was collaborating with Singapore government workforce training and certification programmes for technicians, managers and professional roles.

Note: this predates Microsoft's statement that technicians had used the named GenAI-Powered Maintenance Copilot "since October 2025." The sources do not explain whether the 2024 assistance was a prototype, a different system, or an earlier deployment later renamed or upgraded.
