Intercom charges $0.99 when its AI resolves a ticket. It also charges $0.99 when the AI hands that ticket to a person.

Both are billable outcomes in its own docs. There's a careful line in there: if the customer asks for a human, that's a free escalation. If you configured the agent to route that job to a person, you pay for it.

Now compare. Zendesk bills only for requests resolved without escalation to a human. Decagon and Sierra both say an escalated case isn't charged. Decagon's glossary admits outright that "defining what a resolution is can be tricky."

Which means two vendors quoting you about a dollar a resolution are quoting for different products, and the invoice won't tell you which one you signed.

I don't think Intercom is being sneaky. Pricing the handoff is honest about what these things mostly are, which is a triage layer.

Two questions for any vendor quoting you a per-resolution price: what events trigger a charge, and is an escalation one of them?
