# McDonald's and IBM automated order-taking verification

Captured 2026-09-29.

McDonald's and IBM launch statement, 27 October 2021:
https://corporate.mcdonalds.com/corpmcd/our-stories/article/IBM-McD-Tech-Labs.html

Associated Press report on the end of the test, 18 June 2024:
https://apnews.com/article/mcdonalds-ai-drive-thru-ibm-bebc898363f2d550e1a0cd3c682fa234

The 2021 joint statement said IBM would acquire McD Tech Labs and continue developing
McDonald's automated order-taking technology. It specifically named languages, dialects
and menu variations as integration challenges.

AP verified that McDonald's ended the IBM partnership after testing the system at select
drive-throughs from 2021. McDonald's said it still expected voice ordering to have a
future in its restaurants. AP documented public complaints and videos showing incorrect
orders and reported that unnamed sources told CNBC the system had trouble with accents
and dialects. McDonald's declined to disclose the system's accuracy.

Evidence boundary: the public record verifies that the trial ended and that visible
order errors occurred. It does not establish that accuracy was the reason McDonald's
ended the IBM partnership. It also does not support treating the system as a
retrieval-augmented LLM application. The dominant task was real-time speech recognition,
conversation and transaction entry in a noisy drive-through environment.
