# LinkedIn Help search capture: post character limit

Captured 2026-09-29 from LinkedIn's official Help result:
https://www.linkedin.com/help/talent-insights/answer/a528176

> The character limit for a post is 3,000 characters.

The result was updated five days before capture. LinkedIn also says posts beyond 3,000 characters
should use its article publishing format. This supports a 3,000-character hard platform gate while
keeping 1,400 characters as the nmajor.com normal target.
