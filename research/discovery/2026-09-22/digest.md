# Discovery digest: September 22, 2026

This run scanned Last30Days, Exa, trade press, vendor case studies, company posts, and primary incident reports. The first social pass produced six broad themes, but several were polluted by unrelated high-engagement posts, so this digest keeps only the named cases that survived source checks.

## Top candidates

### 1. Farmers says AI returned 16.4 million hours to its agents

**Source(s):** [Insurance Business](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/technology/how-farmers-used-ai-to-free-up-16-4-million-agent-hours-587592.aspx) · [Farmers Insurance](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/farmers-insurance_how-farmers-used-ai-to-free-up-164-million-activity-7498417219085877249-x04a)

**Relevance 5 · Deep-cut 4 · Breakout 5: spike** · `[named-case]`

Farmers says its two-year program cut routine servicing work by 35% across more than 8,000 agents, reduced new-business calls and chats by 54%, and consolidated more than 300,000 documents into one internal search tool. The numbers are company-reported.

**Read:** [executive interview](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/technology/how-farmers-used-ai-to-free-up-16-4-million-agent-hours-587592.aspx)

### 2. Hirschbach cut days-to-bill from nine days to three

**Source(s):** [Commercial Carrier Journal](https://www.ccjdigital.com/technology/documentation-software/article/15834504/hirschbach-cuts-daystobill-60-with-ai-document-automation)

**Relevance 5 · Deep-cut 5 · Breakout 4: spike** · `[named-case]`

Hirschbach Motor Lines used document AI in its freight paperwork flow and reports a 60% reduction in days-to-bill, 288 manual hours saved each week, and a 70% reduction in manual task hours early in 2026.

**Read:** [trade-press case study](https://www.ccjdigital.com/technology/documentation-software/article/15834504/hirschbach-cuts-daystobill-60-with-ai-document-automation)

### 3. A 60-year-old refinery put AI across 98 processes

**Source(s):** [Asia Business Daily](https://view.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026091510545604231) · [Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/09/15/ai-monitors-98-refinery-processes-round-the-clock-at-sk)

**Relevance 5 · Deep-cut 5 · Breakout 4: spike** · `[named-case]`

SK Innovation is applying AI across 98 processes at its Ulsan refinery while engineers retain the final call. The company is targeting 100 billion won in annual productivity gains by 2030, so the large number is a goal, not a result.

**Read:** [site report](https://view.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026091510545604231)

### 4. GIC kept its 20-year-old ERP and automated the mess around it

**Source(s):** [Prowess Consulting](https://prowessconsulting.com/case-studies/gic-agentic-ai/)

**Relevance 5 · Deep-cut 5 · Breakout 3: baseline** · `[named-case]`

Golden International Corporation's order agent accepts handwritten notes, photos, PDFs, and typed documents, then sends low-confidence matches to a person before writing to the existing ERP. The vendor reports that order entry fell from as much as two hours to about five minutes.

**Read:** [vendor case study](https://prowessconsulting.com/case-studies/gic-agentic-ai/)
