A staffing firm told investors AI had replaced some of its jobs, then softened that wording in the version carrying legal liability.

GEE Group, 12 August. Contract revenue down about 20%, blamed partly on "certain contract staffing jobs being replaced by artificial intelligence."

That sentence sits in the earnings exhibit, which is furnished. The 10-Q filed the same day says AI is "a disruptor of traditional labor and employment markets."

Same company, same morning, two registers. Across all of EDGAR, they're the only filer who used that phrase in the window.
