# Leaked Google DeepMind memo: its own AI CV screener is filtering out qualified candidates

- URL (coverage): https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/gemini/articles/leaked-memo-reveals-google-deepmind-101400670.html
- Published: 2026-08-17 | Originally reported by Bloomberg; this write-up via Futurism/Yahoo Tech
- Accessed: 2026-08-18

## Verbatim
Memo from Google DeepMind's AGI Safety and Alignment Team:
> "We have an applications system with a non-trivial probability your CV will be screened out incorrectly or take too long to reach us."
> "Filling out this form makes sure that a real human on the team will get to see your application."

Google DeepMind spokesperson:
> "This team set up a special form to go past the recruiter review, and get their resumes direct to the people on the team."
> There are "no shortcuts to getting hired."

## Why an executive should care
The team that builds the world's leading AI models does not trust its own employer's AI to read a CV — and built a manual bypass. If your HR function has an AI screener, nobody is auditing what it rejects, because rejections leave no complainant.
