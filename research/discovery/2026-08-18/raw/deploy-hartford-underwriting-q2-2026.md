## Substance

claims operations and various areas in customer experience."

CEO Christopher Swift (Q2 2026 earnings call): "Early results are encouraging with underwriting
activities being completed in a fraction of the time, increasing productivity."

**Key negative signal:** "The Hartford declined to provide specific results on how its AI tools
are performing in its underwriting, claims and customer service areas."

Q2 2026 net income $1.3B (up 30.5% YoY), revenue $7.26B (up 8.1% YoY) — not attributed to AI.
