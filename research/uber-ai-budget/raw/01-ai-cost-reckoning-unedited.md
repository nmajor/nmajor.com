URL: https://finance.yahoo.com/news/companies-expect-double-ai-spending-093825702.html
By David McCann

Survey of 2,360 senior executives worldwide (BCG AI Radar Survey, released January 2026): companies expect to spend approximately **1.7% of their revenue on AI investments this year**, more than double the 0.8% average for 2025.
