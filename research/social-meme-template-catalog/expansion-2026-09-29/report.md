# Meme template expansion, September 29

Added 22 researched contracts. After inspecting every exact render, 13 join the active pool, six remain on hold, and three are rejected. The complete catalog now has **101 contracts: 63 active, 21 hold, 17 rejected**. The previous 79 admission decisions are unchanged.

Active means eligible for draft selection. Every exact background still has `fair-use-review` status. None of this research authorizes publication.

## What changed

The new active IDs are `agnes`, `biw`, `boat`, `gandalf`, `glasses`, `morpheus`, `oprah`, `remembers`, `ski`, `spiderman`, `wddth`, `xy`, and `yodawg`.

The strongest additions cover knowingly false assurances, harmless rebellion, impulsive scope expansion, lost familiarity, changed interpretation, blanket allocation, forgotten practices, local organizational taboos, and recursive delegation. These are useful relationships, not just additional faces for a caption.

Every new contract contains a writing guide, near-miss anti-patterns, renderer-ordered slots with semantic roles and word ceilings, cross-slot invariants, a short operator example, source pointers, origin, safety assessment, visual review, rights status, and admission rationale. Fixed phrases are constrained where needed. The Friends contract requires exact repetition of the first three prompt-response pairs.

The two contact sheets exposed a real renderer defect. `crowd` declares two slots, but the actual JPG places both captions over the same TV screen. Its proposed two-panel interpretation was wrong. It stays on hold. This is precisely why a renderer's line count is not evidence of a meme's visual relationship.

## Evidence and boundaries

The audit began with the [unaltered Memegen response](raw/memegen-templates.json), fetched from [its catalog API](https://api.memegen.link/templates/). It contains 212 rows and 209 unique IDs; `db`, `rollsafe`, and `stonks` each appear twice. These are already contracted assets, so this expansion preserved their existing local art and decisions.

[The source index](raw/index.json) records URLs, redirected URLs, retrieval timestamps, HTTP status, byte counts, and SHA-256 hashes for 25 fetched bodies: 22 candidate pages, a full semantic entry supplementing the Friends photo page, the renderer licence, and Imgflip terms. All fetched successfully. Source pages and licence/terms bodies are unedited. The `.html` extension on the renderer licence is just the capture filename; the body remains plain licence text.

[Candidate examples](raw/candidate-examples.json) preserve each selected Memegen record and matching MemeFact rows without altering the fields. The complete [MemeFact CSV](raw/memefact-templates.csv) and [dataset card](raw/memefact-readme.md) were copied unchanged from the prior audit, not falsely presented as fresh downloads. The CSV's original URL is [the dataset file](https://huggingface.co/datasets/sergiogpinto/memefact-templates/resolve/main/imkg_final_final_final_processor.csv). Generated visual descriptions and writing advice in that dataset are hypotheses, not authority. Many popular captions are off-pattern, political, or unsuitable for this account.

[Candidate evidence](candidate-evidence.json) records three representative uses per template, their source family, exact renderer role mapping, final decision, and visual findings. These are descriptions of historical uses, not captions to copy. Some uses are the original scene or a documented variation rather than three independently captioned image macros; the evidence does not pretend otherwise. The parent audit's [report](../report.md) and [source index](../raw/semantic-sources/index.json) remain the basis for the existing 79 contracts.

This is a semantic and operational audit. No current engagement rankings, algorithm advantage, or improved reach are claimed. Recognizability comes from documented reuse and established formats, not a fresh popularity experiment.

## Visual verification

All 22 operator examples were rendered with the hosted Memegen renderer. [Sheet one](rendered/contact-sheet-01.jpg) and [sheet two](rendered/contact-sheet-02.jpg) were inspected at their 360px-wide tile size, with an additional full-size inspection of the overlapping crowd captions. [Render hashes](rendered/render-hashes.json) preserve the exact inspected files.

The 13 active examples have readable captions and correct roles. `glasses` puts both labels on the right of the matching pose. `spiderman` labels the left and right figures. `wddth` has a top context line and a lower fixed subtitle. The three rejected templates were rendered solely for catalog inspection; they remain unavailable to campaign generation.

The sheets are contact-sheet checks, not a claim that every future caption will be legible. The per-slot limits and per-campaign inspection still apply. No posting approvals were added, and no LinkedIn post or weekly-run file was edited.

## Rights

No authoritative compatible licence for any of the 22 exact background images was found. The [renderer licence](raw/memegen-license.html) covers software and documentation. The MemeFact card's dataset licence does not clear film frames, television stills, promotional portraits, photographs, or artist comics. [Imgflip terms](raw/imgflip-terms.html) do not confer ownership of linked art.

Known provenance is recorded without pretending to resolve the rights chain: Bjork's music video for `boat`, Marvel film or TV frames for several reaction templates, a Matrix frame for `morpheus`, Oprah television footage, South Park and Family Guy frames, Xzibit promotional photography, Allie Brosh's comic for `xy`, Gudim's comic for `drowning`, and source-described photographs for the animal and real-person templates. Publication needs its own exact-asset decision or a separately documented owned recreation. Safety rejection is not cured by a licence.

## Implementation and checks

`sync_templates.py` now accepts repeated `--id` arguments and an optional preserved `--catalog-file`. Selective sync verifies hashes and line counts for retained assets, rejects uncontracted IDs and conflicting selected metadata, and downloads only requested contracted images. The initial full-catalog fetch encountered conflicting duplicate metadata for an existing ID. Restricting conflict checks to selected downloads and using the preserved snapshot makes this bounded expansion reproducible without replacing existing art.

Validation commands:

```bash
mise exec -- python .skills/social-meme-campaign/scripts/validate_template_catalog.py --contracts-only
mise exec -- python .skills/social-meme-campaign/scripts/validate_template_catalog.py
mise exec -- python research/social-meme-template-catalog/expansion-2026-09-29/test_incremental_sync.py
mise exec -- python research/social-meme-template-catalog/expansion-2026-09-29/verify_expansion.py
```

The offline test suite covers one-download selective sync, unchanged retained bytes, rejection of an uncontracted ID, rejection of a changed retained image, and rejection of conflicting selected metadata. Four tests cover those assertions. The prior manifest is preserved in [the baseline capture](raw/baseline-manifest.json), allowing all 79 retained hashes to be compared with the final catalog.

Rendering uses the existing renderer script with `--output-dir research/social-meme-template-catalog/expansion-2026-09-29/rendered`, `--page-size 12`, and one `--id` per candidate listed in `raw/index.json`. Contact-sheet admission labels were refreshed from the final decisions using the unchanged rendered images. Python ran through mise; Pillow 11.3.0 was installed into that Python environment because the renderer's image-library prerequisite was missing. No model inference API was used.
