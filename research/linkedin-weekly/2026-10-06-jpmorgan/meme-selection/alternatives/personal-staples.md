J.P. Morgan's AI deployment includes a robot that removes staples.

The bank described it in May. At one payment-processing site, the robot opens envelopes, unfolds the contents, separates pages and scans the paperwork. J.P. Morgan processed more than 130 million checks in 2025, so the physical handling still matters.

The robot feeds a processing platform rebuilt in 2020. That platform uses AI to extract data and check quality. The bank later added language models for more complex review tasks.

J.P. Morgan says data entry that once required roughly 13 billion keystrokes a year is now largely automated. That's a result attributed to the wider processing system, with no public measurement method. One robot doesn't get the credit for all of it.

The deployment joins the envelope, the document and the payment record. Even good document software needs something to get the staple out.
