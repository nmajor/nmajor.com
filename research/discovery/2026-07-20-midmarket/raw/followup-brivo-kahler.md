## Why this matters for the beat

The original entry's stated risk was "no detail on ... who maintains it." Four to six
months later the person who built it — an executive, not a maintainer — left the
company entirely, to build something new somewhere else. That is the second-bill
question landing in real time, and the honest framing is that **nobody has said what
happened to the tool.** That silence is the finding; do not assert the tool broke or
was abandoned, because no source says so.
