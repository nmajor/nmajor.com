# Hook alternatives

The scheduler-facing files contain the recommended bodies. These are the retained runner-up hooks; none is a separate post.

Materially different candidate-2 bodies live under `variants/` for Rockwell and Domtar. Each has a
different rendered meme relationship under `variants/images/`. FavTrip, Odyssey and STG have hook
alternatives only because another full body would have repeated the same point.

## Rockwell companion

- Recommended: "Rockwell says it cut average machine repair time from 18 minutes to 12."

- Interface: "A maintenance answer is less useful when the technician has to leave the broken machine to find it."
