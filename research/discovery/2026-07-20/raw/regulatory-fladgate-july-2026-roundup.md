# AI Regulatory Actions and Investigations (July 2026)

## UK

**CMA Conduct Requirement on Google**
- Regulator: Competition and Markets Authority (CMA)
- Company: Google
- Action: Conduct requirement imposed on Google's search business
- Details: "lets publishers opt out of having their content power AI features such as AI Overviews and AI Mode — and opt out of their content being used for fine-tuning Google's models"
- Compliance Timeline: Nine months to comply
- Reference: Google's designation with strategic market status in general search

**Ofcom Investigation of X's Grok**
- Regulator: Ofcom
- Company: X (formerly Twitter)
- AI System: Grok chatbot
- Action: "formal investigation into a generative AI chatbot" - first national regulator to do so
- Status: Enforcement record noted; investigation opened

**High Court Rebuke of AI Use**
- Jurisdiction: High Court (UK)
- Action: Judge criticized an associate for using AI to prepare legal submissions
- Issue: Warning against outsourcing "the thinking" to chatbots
