intended to provide coverage for certain exposures."

## Why it matters
The mechanism: a company's AI misconduct that is NOT covered by its own policies still reaches the
D&O tower via a follow-on breach-of-fiduciary-duty suit against the board. That is a board-level
insurance gap, not a tech problem.
