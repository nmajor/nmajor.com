## 6. PepsiCo — digital twin of a plant before spending capex
- https://www.pymnts.com/innovation/2026/pepsico-tests-its-factories-in-software-before-steel/ — PYMNTS, 11 August 2026

Extracted:
- Builds digital twins of factories before physical expansion: "recreating machines, conveyors, pallet routes, and operator paths." AI agents then simulate, test and refine layout changes before any physical modification.
- Vendors: Siemens (Digital Twin Composer) running on Nvidia Omniverse.
- Decision change: "Instead of assuming new physical assets are needed for capacity targets, PepsiCo now identifies existing capacity within current facilities before building new infrastructure."
- Numbers: 20% throughput increase at initial deployment sites; 90% of potential design issues identified before physical changes; estimated 10–15% reduction in capital expenditure; a 12-week pilot replacing months of traditional facility planning.
- Ramon Laguarta, PepsiCo Chairman & CEO: "The scale and complexity of PepsiCo's business, from farm to shelf, is massive, and we are embedding AI throughout"
- Jensen Huang, Nvidia: "Physical industries are entering the age of AI. For companies with real-world assets, digital twins are the foundation of their AI journey."

Confidence: MEDIUM. All numbers are company/vendor-reported and the framing is Nvidia/Siemens-adjacent. Pilots limited to select US facilities.
