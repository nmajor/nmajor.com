## SOURCE 3 — The D&O Diary, "AI-Related Securities Litigation Continues to Evolve"
URL: https://www.dandodiary.com/2026/07/articles/artificial-intelligence/ai-related-securities-litigation-continues-to-evolve/
Publication: The D&O Diary (Kevin LaCroix)
Date published: 2026-07-13
Date accessed: 2026-08-18

Verbatim / extracted:

- Cases discussed: ZoomInfo Technologies, Inc.; Reddit.
- ZoomInfo suit: "Filed June 25, 2026, in the Western District of Washington; class period
  November 3, 2025–May 11, 2026"
- On the shift in theory: early cases focused on overstated capabilities — "AI washing." The
  ZoomInfo complaint represents a new phase: "the company accurately described its AI
  initiatives but failed to disclose that AI was simultaneously disrupting its legacy
  business model."
- "The plaintiffs do not contend that these AI products failed to exist or that the company
  fabricated its AI initiatives."
- Alleged omission: "the effect AI was having on Zoom's legacy business," specifically that
  "customers increasingly were moving away from traditional seat-based subscription models
  toward consumption-based pricing."
- Reddit litigation "reflects concern about how AI-related developments could affect its
  monetization strategy and long-term business model."

CAVEAT: the extraction refers to the defendant variously as "ZoomInfo" and "Zoom." Verify the
exact defendant entity against the docket before naming it in published copy.

## SOURCE 5 — The D&O Diary, "Another AI Spending-Related Securities Class Action" (Rackspace)
URL: https://www.dandodiary.com/2026/08/articles/artificial-intelligence/another-ai-spending-related-securities-class-action/
Publication: The D&O Diary
Date published: 2026-08-05
Date accessed: 2026-08-18

Verbatim / extracted:

- Defendant: Rackspace Technology, Inc., its CEO and CFO.
- Filed: July 29, 2026. Court: Southern District of New York.
- Class period: not stated in the post (must be confirmed from the complaint).
- Allegations: company "failed to disclose the financial and operational consequences of its
  enterprise AI strategy"; "failed to disclose the extent to which its AI strategy would
  require additional capital investment"; investors were "not adequately informed about the
  financial tradeoffs and operational consequences" of aggressive AI investment.
- Stock drop: 33.6% decline following revised guidance announced July 9, 2026.
- Commentary: part of "a growing wave of AI-related securities litigation"; "AI-related risks
  may arise not only from what companies say about AI, but also from what they disclose about
  the costs and consequences." D&O underwriters advised to evaluate whether AI strategies rest
  on realistic assumptions and whether boards align planning, guidance and disclosure.
