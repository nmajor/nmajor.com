Branch's AI claim-reporting calls take about seven minutes.

Voice-AI vendor Liberate says that compares with more than twelve minutes at insurer Branch's outsourced call center. Its September 30 release reports a 42% reduction in reporting time. It says nothing about how quickly Branch settles the claim.

Branch uses Liberate's voice AI and digital intake to collect the first report of a loss. The vendor also describes round-the-clock service, recording actions in insurers' core systems and escalation to people when needed.

I'd judge an intake agent on the record it leaves for the claims team as well as the call duration. Liberate hasn't published the sample or measurement period behind Branch's figure, or Branch-specific escalation results.
