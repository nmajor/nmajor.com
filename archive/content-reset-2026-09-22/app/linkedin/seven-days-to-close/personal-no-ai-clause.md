A firm selling "100% human-written, never AI" research turned out to be entirely AI.

Its eight listed PhDs don't exist. Another part of the site used real people's LinkedIn photos, and one of them still had the open-to-work badge showing.

The sales emails were AI as well.

Plenty of companies are adding a "no AI used" clause to supplier contracts this quarter. It's a cheap sentence to write and almost nobody can audit it.

I'd rather ask a supplier what they do use, and listen for whether the answer gets specific.
