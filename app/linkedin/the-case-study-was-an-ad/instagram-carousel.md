Read the footer before you quote the number.

The most-shared cautionary tale about building your own software with AI is published on a site where every byline belongs to staff at a low-code and AI development agency, and the footer on every page advertises that agency. It opens by referring back to a post that doesn't exist.

I still think the piece is worth reading. It's the only writing anyone points to that puts a real number on the part everybody skips: what the thing costs six months later. But it's being passed around as the neutral warning, and it isn't neutral.

Swipe for the whole thing. Then I went back and checked the success stories I'd cited myself, and the numbers moved between tellings there too.
