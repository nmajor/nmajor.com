# KPMG Global AI Pulse Q2 2026: 49% of leaders scaled back AI agent deployments over cost

- URL: https://www.forbes.com/sites/sandycarter/2026/08/09/kpmg-says-nearly-half-of-executives-pulled-back-ai-agents-over-cost/
- Publication: Forbes (contributor: Sandy Carter)
- Date published: 2026-08-09
- Date accessed: 2026-08-18
- Headline: "KPMG Says Nearly Half Of Executives Pulled Back AI Agents Over Cost"

## Verbatim extract

Source of data: KPMG's Global AI Pulse, Q2 2026.
Sample: 2,145 senior leaders across 20 countries at organizations with $50M+ annual revenue.

Key finding, as worded by the article:
"49 percent of leaders said they had scaled back AI agent deployments because operating costs
outweighed the benefits."

Other figures reported:
- 79% of leaders still view AI as a top investment priority.
- Average AI spending remains steady at $188 million.
- Only 26% of leaders at billion-dollar companies report "full, real-time visibility into what
  AI costs to run at scale".
- One-third cite limited understanding of token pricing as a barrier to agent deployment.

Framing: the article frames this not as an AI bubble but as market maturation — companies
implementing financial discipline rather than abandoning AI entirely.
