# AI security-robot pilots quietly ending across US cities — named vendors, named customers, documented cancellations

- URL: https://www.404media.co/the-roboguard-revolution-is-short-circuiting/
- Publication: 404 Media | Byline: Rebecca Plevin and Varsha Bansal | Published: 2026-08-10
- Accessed: 2026-08-18

## Named vendors and named customers (documented)
Knightscope (K5 robots):
> New York City — Times Square subway station pilot expired 2024. Robot "was reportedly gathering dust in an empty storefront."
> Dublin, Ohio — DubBot program ended after 10 months of a two-year pilot. City spokeswoman stated it "did not fully meet our operational needs" and "failed to identify any criminal incidents or lead to any tickets or arrests."
> San Antonio International Airport — one-month test in 2024 revealed the robot "struggled with badge scanning, communication, and finding its way around."

Daxbots (Oregon-based):
> Salem, Oregon — three-robot parking garage pilot ended after three months in early 2026
> Tempe, Arizona — contract ended; viral video showed robot being outwitted by a visitor

Boston Dynamics robotic dogs:
> NYPD — lease ended in 2021 following public backlash

## Expert quotes
> "A robot can't do that" (de-escalating situations) — Sheila Sparks, Sparks Protection Services
> "AI and robots for security have had a long problematic history. I do not see this changing anytime soon." — Missy Cummings, roboticist, George Mason University
> "Boots on the ground is always better." — Steve Rosta, Direct Approach Security Services

## Status
- DOCUMENTED: the cancellations and the official reasons, sourced to city spokespeople and public records.
- NOTE ON DATES: the ARTICLE is 2026-08-10, but several individual pilot failures date to 2024, 2021 and early 2026. This is a synthesis piece, not a single new incident. Do not present the underlying cancellations as "this week."

## Why an executive should care
Nobody issues a press release when a pilot dies. The reason Dublin, Ohio gave is the one worth repeating in your own steering committee: it "failed to identify any criminal incidents." Ten months, zero results, and it took a reporter to surface it.
