## Verification notes
Company-reported in an SEC-filed earnings press release and repeated on the earnings call — the
strongest class of evidence available short of an audit. Caveats worth flagging: it is a PILOT on
"selected" loans, "ready to close" is not the same as closed, and the 30-60 day baseline is an
asserted "industry norm," not the bank's own measured before-state.
