AI has the potential to transform productivity and growth. To really achieve this potential, however, companies need broad, deep and scaled adoption. At BNY, we are encouraged by what we have already seen AI do and are systematically investing in upskilling our employees and deploying AI throughout the company, all with strong governance in mind. Today, nearly all our employees are trained to use our enterprise AI platform, Eliza, with the majority of our AI builders now coming from outside of our engineering teams. [...]

We then gave our employees a variety of fun and collaborative opportunities to work with each other to explore how AI could add value to their specific roles or teams. AI hackathons and bootcamps are happening across the company. Eliza adoption is up 175% this year, with 99% of employees fully trained and onboarded onto the platform — up from just over a third in January.

SCALING AI ENABLEMENT THROUGH ACCESS, SKILL-BUILDING AND COMMUNITY [...]
AI ACCESS — State-of-the-art capabilities. Objective: Secure, unified AI access so teams can integrate it in everyday work. How: Single enterprise AI platform with secure chat, search, coding and agents [...]

When BNY began its AI journey, we first focused on raising awareness and familiarity. We promoted Eliza and its capabilities: we hosted internal events, demos and teach-ins geared toward helping our people understand what AI is, how we built Eliza and how they could use it. [...]
