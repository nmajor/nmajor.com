Thousands of vendors now sell "AI agents." Gartner's estimate is that only about 130 are legitimate.

That's Gartner's estimate, as reported by Forbes. Most are rebranded chatbots and RPA with a new label on the invoice. Gartner has called the practice "agent washing," and it predicts over 40% of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by the end of 2027, mostly over escalating costs and business value nobody can locate.
