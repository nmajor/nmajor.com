I went back and checked the AI savings numbers everyone is quoting, including two I'd quoted myself. Here are the 7 checks I now run before I believe one.

Every one of them comes from a story that failed it.

Check 1 asks who published it. The most-shared cautionary tale about building your own turned out to be on a site where every byline belongs to staff at a low-code and AI development agency.

Check 3 asks whether the number matches in every telling. One school district's saving reads $250k in April, $220k in May, $250k again in late May, and no dollar figure at all in a fourth interview.

Check 4 asks what the saving is a percentage of. That same board approved a $4.8 million four-year Apple lease over roughly the same stretch. The saving everyone wrote about is around 4.6% of one hardware contract.

Check 7 is the one that stopped me. Is there a number in it dated after year one? Not in a single case I looked at. Including mine.

None of this means the projects failed. It means the evidence you're deciding on is thinner than it looks, and the people holding the real numbers have no reason to publish them.

The full 7 are in the document above. Save it for your next build-versus-buy meeting.
