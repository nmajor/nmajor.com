# Pitch-stage focus group — Codex seat

Date: 2026-09-22

Persona: skeptical insurance COO responsible for service operations, risk, and AI investment.

Provider: OpenAI through the Codex subscription. No API key was used.

## Recommended slate

1. **M1 — The ROI dashboard manufactures its own savings number.** Google Gemini Enterprise treats a thumbs-up, or no follow-up within 30 seconds, as a successful answer; it then assumes three minutes saved and multiplies by employee cost. This is a concrete way to show how an apparently precise AI ROI number can be constructed from assumptions.
2. **W1 — A support assistant with a measured frontline effect.** A peer-reviewed field deployment covering 5,172 support agents reported 15% more resolved chats per hour, with the largest gains among less-experienced agents, and nearly 25% fewer customer requests for a manager from a roughly 6% baseline. The evidence is stronger than a vendor testimonial, while still requiring careful wording about requests rather than completed escalations.
3. **C1 — Deflection without refusal accuracy is incomplete evidence.** An enterprise RAG benchmark covering 983 cases found the best tested system refused correctly only 42.7% of the time and recognized conflicting sources 44.3% of the time. Farmers' 60% deflection claim does not disclose correctness, refusal, or escalation rates.
4. **H1 — Farmers' AI story has a 2003 precedent.** LPL Financial deployed natural-language search to 4,300 representatives and 2,100 branches to deflect service-center calls. The work depended on months of language tuning and review of failed queries, making this a useful counterweight to the idea that conversational internal search is a new operating model.

## Why these four

Together they cover four distinct decisions: how an ROI number is produced, whether a support assistant changes frontline performance, what a deflection metric omits, and what twenty years of precedent says about the operating work behind internal search. H1 has the strongest breakout potential because it challenges the novelty of the Farmers story without claiming the tool is useless.

## Main objection

The set could read as selective skepticism. Each post should state exactly what its evidence does and does not establish about Farmers, and should not imply that weak public measurement proves weak business value.
