## Klarna (named org, but XL enterprise and over-covered)
CEO Sebastian Siemiatkowski, May 2025, reported by Bloomberg/Reuters. As quoted downstream:
"From a brand perspective, a company perspective, I just think it's so critical that you are clear to your customer that there will be always a human if you want."
And the framing that "investing in the quality of human support is a wise way forward" for premium tiers.
Useful corrective found at https://getperspective.ai/blog/klarna-ai-customer-service-replacing-700-agents-conversational-ai-case-study (vendor blog — treat as commentary, not source):
"Klarna did not reverse its AI strategy — it rebalanced it... The AI assistant remained the front line for the high-volume tier; humans were reintroduced for the premium and complex-case tier where AI parity hadn't held."
<-- If Klarna is used at all, this nuance matters. The popular "Klarna fired 700 people and had to hire them all back" version is wrong.
