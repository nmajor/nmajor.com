Google counts 30 seconds of silence as a successful AI answer.

Gemini Enterprise's docs count a thumbs-up as success. They also count no feedback and no follow-up question within 30 seconds. Each successful query gets three minutes of saved time, which the dashboard multiplies by a wage supplied by the customer.

That may be a reasonable planning estimate. The dashboard never observes whether the employee saved three minutes, or whether silence meant the answer worked.

I wouldn't call the resulting dollar figure a before-and-after measurement of work.
