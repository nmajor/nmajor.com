The best model in a new enterprise RAG test refused only 42.7% of questions whose answers were missing.

The EnterpriseRAG preprint started with 491 production queries and built 983 test cases around missing, irrelevant, or conflicting documents. No model recognized conflicting evidence more than 44.3% of the time.

Farmers was not part of the benchmark. These were deliberately difficult test cases, so they tell us nothing about Farmers' actual accuracy.

Farmers says its bot resolves more than 60% of questions that used to become service-center calls. It has not published correctness, refusal, or escalation figures. A call can disappear even when the answer is wrong, and the disclosed number cannot separate the two.
