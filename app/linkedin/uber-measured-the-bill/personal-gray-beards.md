"Mistakenly we thought that by just introducing artificial intelligence... that would produce a high-quality product." That's a Ford VP.

Charles Poon, Ford's VP of vehicle hardware engineering, was explaining why the company rehired roughly 350 veteran "gray beard" engineers in June, some ex-Ford, some from suppliers, after its AI-run quality control kept missing targets. The same week, on June 25, J.D. Power ranked Ford the No. 1 mainstream brand for initial quality, the first time since 2010. Maybe related, maybe not. 

Ford kept the automations running. The rehired veterans are retraining the younger engineers and reprogramming the AI quality tools that missed the targets. That's as far as the story has gotten.
