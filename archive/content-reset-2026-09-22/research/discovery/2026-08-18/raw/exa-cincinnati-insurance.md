# RAW — Cincinnati Insurance automating manual data entry
Accessed: 2026-08-18. Lane: Exa neural search (insurer workflow intent).
Source: https://www.dig-in.com/news/cincinnati-insurance-is-automating-away-manual-data-entry — published 2026-07-27 (Digital Insurance / Arizent trade press)

## Extracted facts
- Deployed: Acord Transcriber (ML + LLMs) to extract data from insurance documents. Vendor: Acord Solutions Group.
- Workflows: sales, claims, commercial lines, and Cincinnati Specialty Underwriters (CSU).
- Numbers: ~90% time savings on data entry; 90% reduction in AI platform development time; covers 800+ Acord forms across 4,700+ versions; time to build Acord-form-reading capability cut from 8 months to under 1 month.
- Quoted: Frank Neugebauer, VP and Generative AI Director, The Cincinnati Insurance Companies; Rakesh Tangri, SVP and Global Chief Solutions Architect, Acord Solutions Group.
- Stated problem: rekeying created transcription errors hurting data quality; keeping form versions current at scale is "critical, but challenging".
- Caveat: numbers are company/vendor reported; no independent audit.
