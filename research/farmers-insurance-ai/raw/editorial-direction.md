# Editorial direction from Nicholas Major

Date: 2026-09-22

Farmers Insurance is the topic for the newsletter.

The essay should be concise and punchy, using the nmajor.com writing voice together with
the unslop rules. It should explain what Farmers did well and why that produced such a
strong impact. It must not turn the case study into prescriptive advice, a diagnostic
question for the reader, a "lesson," or a "do this, not that" conclusion. Let readers
piece together the implications themselves.

The intended closing register is a direct observation along these lines: this is a
perfect example of the depth of research required to identify where AI can actually make
a difference.

The same research packet and writing prompt will be given to Kimi K3, MiniMax M3, and GLM
5.3 Flash. Their drafts will be presented anonymously for a blind preference test.

## Direction after the blind test

Nick selected GLM 5.3 Flash as the default newsletter writer until he says otherwise. He
rejected the first framing after noticing that the only concrete AI product Farmers has
described is an internal support-search assistant. The revised piece should contrast that
modest implementation with the company's 16.4 million-hour story, while acknowledging that
similar support-search tools can produce meaningful returns when they work.

Nick's phrasing for the social angle is: "Hey, you want to look good? Generate a support bot,
and then talk about all the time you save" and "How you're revolutionizing the industry with
AI."
