Thousands of vendors now sell "AI agents." Gartner can find about 130 actually building the real thing.

That's Gartner's estimate, as reported by Forbes. Most of the rest are rebranded chatbots and RPA with a new label on the invoice. Gartner named the practice "agent washing," and it predicts over 40% of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by the end of 2027, mostly over escalating costs and business value nobody can locate.

I run agents every working day, real ones that take actions and get reviewed before anything ships. So here's the two-question test I'd take into any vendor demo. What can it do without a human clicking approve, and what happens when it's wrong? If the answers are "nothing" and "it can't be," you're being offered last year's chatbot at this year's price.
