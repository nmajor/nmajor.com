## Facts (WebSearch synthesis)
- The Regional Court of Munich prohibited Google from spreading false claims about two Munich-based publishers (Verlagshaus24 and GeraMond Verlag) through its AI-based search overviews.
- Google's AI Overview falsely linked Verlagshaus24 to scams, subscription traps, and "dubious"/sketchy business practices.
- Per the court, the AI mixed up information about other, genuinely sketchy companies with the plaintiffs and drew connections that did not appear in ANY of the linked sources — i.e. pure invention.
- The court classified Google as a DIRECT infringer ("unmittelbarer Störer") because the AI Overview is Google's OWN content, not merely a list of search results. AI Overviews generate "independent, new, and substantive statements" by evaluating and combining content from third-party sites.
- Google's defense — that users should fact-check, and that users generally know "that information generated with AI should not be blindly trusted" — was REJECTED.
- Court held the summaries are "above all an expression of Google's business activities."
- Penalty for violation: up to EUR 250,000 or detention. Google ordered to cover 80% of procedural costs.
- Google has announced it will appeal.

## Schneier/Sanders framing (The Guardian, 24 June 2026)
"AI agents are agents of the person or organization that deploys them—and should be treated by the law as such. If a company hired human writers to write its summaries, that company would be liable for inaccuracies in those summaries. If a company's human agent signed contracts in the company's name, that company would be bound by those contracts."
"To allow businesses to hide behind the excuse of faulty AI in those same circumstances would be a massive handout to companies, and would introduce disastrous incentives for corporate misbehavior."
"Companies may not be able to profitably operate AI lawyers, doctors and media influencers if they are held responsible for what they say and do. We're OK with this outcome."

- Defendant is tech-native (Google) — OUT of the strict lane — but the VICTIMS are two named, established, non-tech publishers, and the holding is the doctrinal engine behind every non-tech deployer's exposure.
