## Substance

From Digital Insurance, 2026-08-13:
- "approximately $213 million in enterprise AI value by the end of 2025"
- projects "$717 million in enterprise AI value by 2027"
- AI "eliminated more than 400,000 manual intake and indexing tasks" in U.S. claims
- "developers using GitHub Copilot increased productivity approximately 30%"

From Manulife's own release, 2026-06-16 (figures appear to be CAD vs the USD above):
- "AI initiatives are expected to generate over $1 billion in enterprise value by 2027", with
  "$300 million" achieved by year-end 2025
- value defined as "realized run-rate expense reductions, top-line revenue uplift from AI-powered
  workflows, fraud reduction, and growth absorption"
- MAUDE (Manulife Automated Underwriting Decision Engine), Canada: "processes more than half of
  eligible individual life insurance applications automatically, delivering decisions in as little
  as two minutes for qualified applicants"
- "41% year-on-year increase" in AI talent pool
- Ranked #1 life insurer, 3rd overall, in the 2026 Evident AI Index
- Rolling Microsoft 365 Copilot to 30,000+ employees; deploying Microsoft Agent 365 for governance

## Verification notes
Company-reported in investor materials and a press release; two different currency framings of the
same programme ($213M/$717M USD vs $300M/$1B CAD) is worth noting. "Enterprise AI value" is a
self-defined composite metric that mixes cost savings with revenue uplift and "growth absorption" —
not an audited P&L line.
