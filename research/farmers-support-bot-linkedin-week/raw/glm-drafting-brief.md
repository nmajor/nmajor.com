# Drafting brief for four unapproved LinkedIn posts

Return JSON only with a `posts` array. Each item must contain `angle`, `offsetDays`, `hooks` (four genuinely different options), `selectedHook`, `body`, and `visualRecommendation`.

Write as Nicholas Major for skeptical owners, CEOs, CIOs, and COOs at medium-to-large traditional businesses. The goal is to build trust in his practical judgment about AI. No hard sell.

Hard rules:

- Four posts, assigned offsets 1, 2, 3, and 6.
- Each post is 100 to 180 words, no more than 1,400 characters.
- The selected hook is the first paragraph and no more than 140 characters.
- No hashtags, em dashes, emojis, title-case headings, or external link in the body.
- No rule-of-three cadence, broetry, generic lesson, advice, reader prescription, or closing question.
- Never use `the useful part`, `the interesting part`, `the best part`, `the important part`, or any evaluative `the X part` preamble.
- Never use `revolutionize`, `leverage`, `unlock`, `transform`, `landscape`, `robust`, `seamless`, or other marketing language.
- Do not use `not X, but Y`, `not just X`, or a tidy callback ending.
- State what happened, what the evidence supports, and what it does not support. Let the reader infer the takeaway.
- Use contractions and uneven paragraph lengths. One dry aside across the whole batch is enough.
- First person only for an honest reaction or judgment, never invented experience.
- Name sources in the body where natural. Put source URLs in an HTML comment after the visible body.
- Do not imply any finding proves something about Farmers unless it directly does.

Use these four angles and no others:

1. `value-dashboard`, offset 1. Google's Gemini Enterprise analytics count an answer as successful when the user gives a thumbs-up or does not ask another question within 30 seconds. Google assigns three minutes saved to each success and multiplies it by a customer-supplied hourly wage. Source: https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/view-analytics

2. `support-agent-study`, offset 2. A peer-reviewed Quarterly Journal of Economics field deployment followed 5,172 support agents. Resolved chats per hour rose 15%, with the largest gains among less-experienced agents. Customer requests to speak to a manager fell almost 25% from a roughly 6% baseline. The study measured requests, not completed escalations. Source: https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjae044

3. `rag-refusal`, offset 3. A 2026 EnterpriseRAG benchmark constructed 983 enterprise-style cases from production RAG queries. The best tested system correctly refused only 42.7% of missing-knowledge cases and recognized document conflicts 44.3% of the time. Farmers reports more than 60% call deflection but does not publish correctness, refusal, or escalation figures. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11584 and https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/technology/how-farmers-used-ai-to-free-up-16-4-million-agent-hours-587592.aspx

4. `search-bot-2003`, offset 6. In 2003, LPL Financial put natural-language search in front of portal content for 4,300 representatives across 2,100 branches. The goal was to reduce service-center and supervisor calls. LPL spent months tuning company language and fed failed searches into its content library. This is the strongest breakout candidate because the operating model behind the supposedly new AI use case is 23 years old. Source: https://www.computerworld.com/article/1339548/enterprises-widen-the-search-net.html

Visual recommendations must be honest. Use `none` when a visual would decorate rather than clarify. At most two posts should recommend a meme. For a meme, give a classic-template concept and exact short slot text, but do not assume rights are cleared.
