Someone hid white three-point text inside a court filing. It told any AI that read the document to side with him.

A clerk noticed the strange gap on the page. The judge then wrote fourteen pages and barred him from filing electronically. The injection itself failed.

Every document that arrives at your company now passes somewhere near a model. Invoices, CVs, claims, supplier forms, insurance paperwork.

Any of them can carry instructions your people will never see.
