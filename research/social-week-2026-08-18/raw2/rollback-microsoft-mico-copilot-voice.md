# Microsoft pulls Mico out of Copilot Voice after under a year

- URL: https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/08/14/less-than-a-year-on-microsoft-tells-mico-to-pipe-down/5287793
- Publication: The Register
- Date published: 2026-08-14 (Friday)
- Date accessed: 2026-08-18
- Headline: "Less than a year on, Microsoft tells Mico to pipe down"
- Subhead: "Copilot's gurning blob is leaving Voice for a new career in education"

## Verbatim extract

Confirmed change: Microsoft is removing Mico from Copilot Voice. Users still access voice
features, but the anthropomorphic character no longer appears. Mico is repositioned to
"Learn Live", described as "a voice-based study mode that guides users through subjects and
assignments."

Microsoft statements (CONFIRMED):
"Mico helped us learn about warmth, expressiveness, and how people want to talk with AI."
"Learn is where the character has the most room to grow, with tutoring sessions that give Mico
more to react to and teach through."

Context: change accompanies Microsoft's merger of its consumer and work Copilot applications.
Mico lasted less than a year in its original role. The Register compares it to Clippy, Cortana
and Microsoft Bob.
