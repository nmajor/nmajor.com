# Banco Popular Dominicano — "AURA" agent ecosystem for operational risk supervision

## Substance

AURA = "an ecosystem of agents built on Microsoft Copilot Studio and Microsoft Power Platform that
automates real-time risk monitoring and integrates data, processes, and controls in a continuous,
governed model." Described as "a constellation of specialized AI agents that collaboratively
monitor, detect, and escalate operational risks across the bank's sprawling network of branches,
digital channels, and back-office processes."

Numbers:
- "monitors 100% of its operational risk universe" in real time, "up from roughly 40% coverage"
- "seven times greater analytical capacity"
- "reduced manual operating effort by 70%"
- "98% methodological accuracy"
- "approximately 80,000 documents per week and more than 300 cases per day"

Governance: "A governance architecture for complete traceability controls access, differentiates
roles, and monitors every process in real time—including through supervisory agents that oversee
the entire ecosystem." Human-in-the-loop is explicit.

## Verification notes
Vendor case study; no independent confirmation found. The 40% -> 100% coverage before/after is the
unusual part: the claim is about scope of supervision, not headcount cut. Built on low-code
(Power Platform / Copilot Studio) by the bank's own risk team rather than bought as a product.
