"seven times greater analytical capacity" / "reduced manual operating effort by 70%" /
"98% methodological accuracy" / "approximately 80,000 documents per week and more than 300 cases per day"

"reduced planning-phase audit time by 20%" / "accelerated risk and control matrix creation from days to seconds"

"saved 28,000 hours annually" / "32% of employees using self-service analytics" /
"more than 1.4 million in total cost-of-ownership savings over five years"

"sales conversion improvements of up to 57%"

**Cactus Life Sciences** (pharma services) — scientific document review / data extraction:
"deployed more than 30 custom automation agents" / "efficiency improvements of approximately 35% to 50%"

**Grandiose Supermarkets** (grocery) — AI shopping companion:
