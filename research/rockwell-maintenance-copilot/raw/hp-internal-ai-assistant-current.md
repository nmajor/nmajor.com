# HP internal AI assistant case-study capture

Captured 2026-09-29 from:
https://www.anamariazamfirache.com/casestudies/empowering-employees-with-ai-the-product-strategy-behind-an-enterprise-internal-assistant

The original `hp-internal-ai-assistant.html` capture now resolves to a Squarespace
"Website Expired" page. The current page is available at the URL above. Key source text
needed for the counterexample assessment is preserved below.

> HP's internal AI tool launched globally and stalled. I was brought in as Product
> Manager to diagnose why, rebuild the strategy, and drive adoption that actually stuck.

> HP's Digital & Transformation Office rolled out an internal AI assistant to support
> thousands of employees globally — document access, content generation, task automation.
> Post-launch metrics told a different story: low adoption, inconsistent usage, and
> unclear satisfaction.

> Employees opened the tool once and left. The assistant had no awareness of who was
> using it, what they needed, or how to guide them to a useful outcome. Generic UI,
> generic responses — and word spreading through the org that it "doesn't work."

The page says the recovery work used employee interviews across Sales, Marketing, HR and
R&D; a service blueprint; jobs-to-be-done analysis; a prompt and experience audit; guided
onboarding; department personas; templates; prompt suggestions; feedback loops; and a
structured pilot with 200 employees.

Claimed outcomes on the page are 40% adoption growth in Q1, satisfaction moving from 2.8
to 4.1 out of 5 in the pilot group, and 12,000 employees "impacted at scale." The page
does not define adoption, provide absolute user counts, give a before/after period beyond
"Q1," publish underlying analytics, or provide an HP-owned corroborating source.

Source identity and incentive: personal portfolio and consultancy lead-generation page
by Ana Zamfirache. It describes her as the product manager for the work and ends with a
commercial call to book an audit. Treat it as a practitioner account, not an independent
case study or audited HP disclosure.
