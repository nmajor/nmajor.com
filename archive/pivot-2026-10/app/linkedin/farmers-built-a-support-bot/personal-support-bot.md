Want to look like you’re revolutionizing an industry with AI?

Build an internal support bot.

Farmers Insurance put 300,000 internal documents behind a conversational search box. It replaced five search systems and reportedly answers more than 60% of questions that previously became service-center calls.

Then Farmers started making the rounds with a much bigger number: 16.4 million hours saved.

That figure came from a two-year operational overhaul, not just the bot. Farmers hasn’t disclosed how much of the savings the bot produced.

The bot may have a perfectly good ROI. But “we built a useful internal search tool” doesn’t sound quite as revolutionary.
