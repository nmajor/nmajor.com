# PenFed Credit Union — four Agentforce agents in service, collections and IT

- URL: https://diginomica.com/penfeds-ace-encounter-ai-how-one-financial-services-organization-powering-ahead-its-agentic
- Publication: diginomica (independent trade press)
- Author: Stuart Lauchlan
- Date published: 2026-08-11
- Date accessed: 2026-08-18

## Substance

Four agents on Salesforce Agentforce:
1. **Penny** (internal) — IT Service Management agent handling employee requests
2. **Max** (internal) — collections research agent accessing payment histories
3. **Wingman** (internal) — employee service agent supporting Member Service Reps
4. **ACE** (external) — member-facing agent on online banking and mobile

Numbers:
- Penny "deflects about 70% of ITSM cases"; PenFed was able to "turn off our IT phone number
  support line"
- Wingman: "a 10% reduction in handle time" and "reduced back office support calls by 30%"
- Contact centre baseline: "roughly 10,000 calls a day," each requiring "about four minutes of
  manual summarization"
- Scale: "around 2.9 million members", "$31 billion of assets", 2,300 employees

Difficulty noted by exec (Boles): they "spent an enormous amount of time piloting, designing, and
developing" governance structures because of financial services regulation. No cost overruns or
setbacks disclosed.

## Verification notes
Journalist-written from an executive interview; numbers are company-reported to a reporter rather
than filed or audited. The "turned off the IT phone line" detail is a falsifiable, checkable claim.
