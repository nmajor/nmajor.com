If your business carries commercial liability insurance, there's a decent chance an AI exclusion is being added to it this year, and nobody will make a fuss about it.

Verisk and ISO, who write the standard forms most insurers use, published three of these endorsements in January. The wording is broad: it strips cover for injury or damage "arising out of" generative AI. Some insurers went further and wrote their own versions.

The screenshot is one of the actual forms. It's one page, and the part that removes the cover is two sentences long.

The bit I keep chewing on is what it signals. Insurers are the first people in this whole AI cycle who have been asked to price the risk with their own money, and a lot of them have looked at it and said no thanks. That doesn't mean AI doesn't work. It means nobody can yet put a number on how wrong it goes.

These attach when your policy renews, so most people will meet one without ever making a decision about it.
