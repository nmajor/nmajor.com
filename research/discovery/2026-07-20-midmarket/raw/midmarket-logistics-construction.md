Title: AI moving from back office to driver's seat in trucking operations — FreightWaves
Author: Noi Mahoney

Chicago-based Datatruck is an all-in-one TMS built to automate back-office operations for trucking companies and freight brokers. Co-founder and Chief AI Officer Ulugbek Ergashev:

"The company's platform is now processing about 10,000 documents daily, he said, replacing manual workflows that once took several minutes per load. 'Before, it took about three to five minutes. Today it takes about 10 to 15 seconds,' Bek said."

"'Every rate confirmation, every BOL, every POD — our system reads that automatically and extracts the data,' Ergashev told FreightWaves."

## SOURCE: https://www.constructiondive.com/news/gilbane-trunk-tools-ai-baird-center/725378/
Title: How Gilbane used an AI tool to track 21,000 documents
Publication: Construction Dive (TRADE PRESS)
Extracted 2026-07-20 via tavily_extract advanced.

"Indeed, a case study from Trunk Tools showed users asked 246 questions across 37 working days on the Baird Center job. Of those answers, 87% were correct, as validated by the project team. The case study claimed that using TrunkText saved workers between 20 and 40 minutes of search and travel time for each query, and avoided more than $100,000 in rework per month, per the case study."

### Avoiding rework
"Roy pointed to a question the team had about a fireplace in a feature wall with a large exhaust system covered with acoustic plaster. The material for the finish took six months to acquire from Europe. Roy and the mechanical contractor performed an inspection and noticed an anomaly in the ductwork.

They had a question — did the seams need to be sealed? A mistake would mean costly rework, expensive equipment rental fees and lost time. Typically, answering this kind of question would involve a time-consuming email chain between the mechanical contractor, the design team and Roy, which would take hours, if not days.

Instead, they asked TrunkText."

"To underscore the value of those savings, Roy points to how he has traditionally had to hunt for information on projects. Typically, when a question arises, he has to dig into his iPad and manually search for the right documents, or trek back to the jobsite trailer to look at drawings, contracts or other papers for the answer. Along the way, he might get stopped by subcontractors with other questions, or have to deal with a safety issue."

"Instead, TrunkText answered his questions 'within five to 10 seconds,' Roy said."

"Roy said implementation was relatively painless — it took only a day or two to have Trunk Tools linked to Gilbane's Procore and Sharepoint deployments."

NOTABLE: the **87% correct** figure is one of the very few published accuracy/error rates in this whole sweep — i.e. 13% of answers were wrong on a jobsite where wrong answers cause rework. That is the "what broke" number the ICP panel asked for. Gilbane is ~3,000+ staff (ABOVE mid-market band) but the deployment is scoped to ONE job (Baird Center, Milwaukee) and one superintendent's workflow.
