"## CMA Conduct Requirement Overview
The conduct requirement is imposed following the CMA's decision to designate Google with strategic market status (SMS) in general search services. The CMA designated Google as holding strategic market status in October 2025, controlling over 90% of UK searches, which enabled binding conduct requirements without proving illegal behavior.

## Publisher Opt-Out Rights for AI Overviews
In a world first, publishers will now have effective tools to prevent their content being used to power AI features in search, such as AI Overviews. Following consultation feedback, Google will now also have to allow publishers to opt-out of allowing their content to be used for the 'fine-tuning' of AI models.

## Key Requirements
The rules cover three fronts: opting out of AI Overviews, requiring proper attribution with clear links in AI-generated results, and letting publishers block their content from being used to fine-tune Google's AI models. The requirement designates Google as having 'strategic market status' among search engines and directs it to implement a suite of publisher-facing controls, with the main obligations due to take effect in December 2026.

## Implementation Timeline
Google has nine months from June 3, 2026 to comply fully — meaning the complete feature set could arrive as late as March 2027."
