## Verbatim / extracted facts
- Tool: "Automated Counting". Vendor: NomadGo (Redmond, WA).
- Scale: all 11,300 company-operated Starbucks cafes in North America.
- Rollout completed end of September 2025. NomadGo notified of cancellation 2026-04-03. Baristas notified 2026-05-18. ~9 months live.
- Failure modes: reflections on steel fridges doubled carton counts (five became ten); misidentified milk types and syrups; miscounted bins as food items; WiFi disruptions caused incomplete counts with no manual backup option.
- Seasonal packaging / limited-time items required up to six weeks of computer-vision retraining; developers sometimes learned of new products only after shelf placement.
- Backend: "a legacy IBM AS/400 system from the 1990s, which makes real-time store data hard to move."
- Accuracy: 99% in controlled tests.
- Estimated program cost: "north of $10m over several years."
- NomadGo went from ~30 people to a large chunk laid off.
- David Greschler, NomadGo CEO: "a complete surprise"; "there's nothing you can do when leadership and strategy change".
- Starbucks: "We use technology to support human connection, not to replace it"; "When it fell short, we listened to feedback and changed course."
- Replacement: baristas returned to manual hand counts.
