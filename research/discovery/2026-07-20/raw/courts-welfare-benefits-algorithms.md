## https://www.theguardian.com/society/article/2024/jun/23/dwp-algorithm-wrongly-flags-200000-people-possible-fraud-error [2024 — OLD]
More than 200,000 people wrongly faced investigation for housing benefit fraud and error after a government algorithm fell short. Two-thirds of claims flagged as potentially high risk by a DWP automated system over three years were legitimate (FOI figures). ~£4.4m spent on checks that saved no money. Figures first obtained by Big Brother Watch.
Information commissioner inquiry into algorithms used by 11 local authorities: "We have not found any evidence to suggest that claimants are subjected to any harms or financial detriment as a result of the use of algorithms or similar technologies in the welfare and social care sector."

## https://europeanaifund.org/newspublications/how-ai-driven-welfare-systems-are-deepening-inequality-and-poverty-across-europe
Lighthouse Reports: Sweden's Social Insurance Agency (Försäkringskassan) fraud prediction algorithm discriminated against women, migrants, individuals with foreign backgrounds, low-income earners and people without a university education.
France: similar algorithmic system discriminating against the most vulnerable.
Denmark: Amnesty Tech two-and-a-half year investigation — data-driven fraud control algorithms risk discriminating against low-income groups, racialized groups, migrants, refugees, ethnic minorities, people with disabilities, older people; flagging "unusual" living situations (multi-occupancy, intergenerational households, "foreign affiliations") as higher risk = social scoring, prohibited under the EU AI Act.
Dutch toeslagenaffaire: tens of thousands of parents falsely accused of fraud, child benefits suspended, hostile investigations, debt, unemployment, forced evictions, mental health harm.
