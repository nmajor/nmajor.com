# AI-related securities class actions on pace to double in 2026

- Publication: InvestmentNews
- Date published: 2026-07-31
- Date accessed: 2026-08-18
- Underlying report: "Securities Class Action Filings — 2026 Midyear Assessment," Cornerstone Research + Stanford Law School Securities Class Action Clearinghouse (with Stanford Securities Litigation Analytics)

## Extracted numbers

- 15 AI-related securities class action filings in the first half of 2026.
- Annualized pace of 30 filings for the full year — nearly double the 16 filings recorded in all of 2025.
- AI-related cases accounted for $385 billion of the $529 billion total Disclosure Dollar Loss Index.
- Two AI filings alone represented $1.235 trillion (66%) of the Maximum Dollar Loss Index.
- Three securities jury trials in H1 2026 — "the first multiple-trial period since 2009."
- Companies referenced: Elon Musk/Twitter (March 2026 jury trial), Armistice Capital (April 2026, cleared), Exxon Mobil (May 2026, cleared).
