Intercom charges $0.99 when its AI resolves a ticket. It also charges $0.99 when the AI hands that ticket to a person.

There's careful nuance about when an escalation is billable, but the effect is that they charge for plenty of tickets the AI never actually resolves.

Zendesk bills only for requests resolved "without any escalation to a human agent". Decagon and Sierra don't charge for escalated cases at all, and Decagon's own glossary says "defining what a resolution is can be tricky".

The marketing says AI resolves your tickets. The pricing says budget for people to keep finishing the job. I'd trust the pricing.
