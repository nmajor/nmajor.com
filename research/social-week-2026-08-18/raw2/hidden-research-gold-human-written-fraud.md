# "Research Gold": vendor selling "100% human-written, never AI" medical research is entirely AI, with stolen identities

- URL: https://www.404media.co/company-offering-100-human-written-never-ai-peer-review-is-entirely-ai/
- Publication: 404 Media | Byline: Emanuel Maiberg | Published: 2026-08-11
- Accessed: 2026-08-18

## Company
Research Gold — sells medical research services (manuscript drafting, systematic reviews) to researchers.

## Verbatim / documented
> Website claim: "100% human-written, never AI" with PhD reviewers and methodologists
> Eight team members listed on the About page (including "Dr. Elena Vasquez" and "Dr. Mei-Lin Chen") don't exist. Their profile pictures are clearly AI-generated, and searches yield no academic publications or professional footprint matching their stated credentials.
> A separate section listed real methodologists with actual LinkedIn profile photos lifted directly from their accounts — including the "#opentowork" graphic.
> Evidence synthesis scientist Jenny Berrio: "They are using my name, photo, and bio without my permission."
> All customer interactions were AI-generated — phone calls with "Sarah," email responses, and quote generation — despite claims of human expertise.

## Status of claims
- DOCUMENTED: the AI-generated staff photos and non-existent credentials; the lifted LinkedIn photos; Berrio's on-record confirmation that her identity was used without permission; the AI-run sales calls and emails (404 Media interacted with them directly).
- NOT ESTABLISHED: how many customers it has. No evidence of credible researchers actually using the service is documented.

## Why an executive should care
The "human-in-the-loop" and "no AI used" guarantees in your supplier contracts are now the exact claim most worth faking — and the cheapest to fake. This vendor's differentiator WAS the human promise, and every human on the page was fabricated or stolen.
