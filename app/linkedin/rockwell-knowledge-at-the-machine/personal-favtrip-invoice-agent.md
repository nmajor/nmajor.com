A three-store convenience chain says an AI agent caught a $25,000 fuel-invoice error.

FavTrip reconciles more than 4,550 fuel invoices. Owner Babir Sultan started its billing agent with read-only reporting on which loads had been billed, drafted and paid.

Then he let it flag exceptions. Only after that worked did he give it a vetted vendor list and permission to email about mismatches, with Sultan copied. It caught a load billed to the wrong store and got the vendor to correct it within minutes.

The $25,000 figure comes from Sultan in a convenience-store trade publication. There is no independent audit or detail on the exact error, so I would not turn one catch into a general ROI claim.

The clearest win came from checking the bill. Sultan also gave the agent more authority in stages, after he had seen the narrower version work.
