## Observational studies

- `metricool-linkedin-study-2026.pdf` — https://metricool.com/wp-content/uploads/Linkedin-Study-2026-EN.pdf
  - Vendor study of 673,658 posts from 63,108 accounts, comparing Jan–Feb 2025 with Jan–Feb 2026.
  - Observational and commercially produced; useful for broad directional benchmarks, not causal algorithm claims.
- `linkpost-algorithm-study-2026.html` — https://www.linkpost.gg/en/playbooks/linkedin-algorithm-playbook-2026/study
  - Vendor study of 438,413 posts and 5,291,997 comments. The publisher discloses sparse impression data (1.9%), French-language skew, creator opt-in bias, observational design, and a commercial conflict.
