A small insurance account can bring a small premium and a full workload.

XPT Specialty named that problem in its October 1 announcement. Small commercial accounts can take nearly as much processing work as large ones, which limits how widely they get shopped around.

Its new binding tool sends a risk to up to 20 insurance markets in parallel through direct system connections. An XPT specialist reviews the results and remains responsible for final placement.

Retail agents keep their existing workflow. XPT says there's no new portal and no re-keying of the submission.

The company reports growth figures, but doesn't isolate the tools' contribution or disclose their costs. I wouldn't count those figures as proven AI returns.

The operating change is clearer. More of the comparison work moves to the wholesaler, making a broader search more practical for accounts that bring in less revenue.
