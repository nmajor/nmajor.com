# Sainsbury's suspends Facewatch live facial recognition alerts at East Dulwich store

- Publication: The Register
- Date published: 2026-08-17 (Monday)
- Date accessed: 2026-08-18
- Headline: "Sainsbury's staff eject wrong shopper after facial recognition alert – again"

## Verbatim / near-verbatim extract (via WebFetch summary of page)

What happened: Matt Arnold, a 46-year-old comedy promoter, was wrongly ejected from
Sainsbury's East Dulwich store on August 6 after staff responded to a Facewatch facial
recognition alert. He was mistakenly identified as a suspected thief while attempting to
purchase alcohol at self-checkout.

System/vendor: Facewatch live facial recognition (LFR) technology.

Sainsbury's statement (CONFIRMED BY COMPANY):
"The incident was caused by human error, not the facial recognition technology"
"the Facewatch system has a 99.98 percent accuracy rate, and every match is reviewed by a
trained manager."

System status (CONFIRMED): Sainsbury's "temporarily suspended LFR alerts at the store while
it reviewed its internal processes and considered additional staff training."

Context: second such wrongful ejection in 2026, following a similar February incident
involving Warren Rajah. Human error in staff response — not technology malfunction — was the
cited cause in both cases.
