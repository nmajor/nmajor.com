I went looking for one thing: a company that built its own tool with AI, and an honest account of what it cost a year later. Not the launch post. The bill.

I couldn't find one.

The most-shared cautionary tale in the whole category turned out to be published by a development agency whose bylines are its own staff. Then I went back and checked the success stories I'd cited myself, and the numbers moved between tellings.

This week's issue is about why the bill is always missing, and what that means if you're deciding whether to build or buy.
