## VERBATIM EXTRACT — American Banker (via WebFetch)
Bank Name: CB Financial Services Inc. (parent company of Community Bank, Pennsylvania)
AI Tool Used: the article does not name the specific unauthorized AI application the employee accessed.
Data Exposed: Customer names, Social Security numbers, and dates of birth
Key Dates:
- May 5, 2026: Incident discovered
- May 7, 2026: Company determined incident was material
- May 11, 2026: SEC Form 8-K filed
- June 8, 2026: Article published

Bank Executive Quotes:
John H. Montgomery (President and CEO): "There is no evidence the data was misused" and the bank believed it "reached the AI vendor in time to stop the customer information from being used to train the vendor's model."
Jennifer George (Senior EVP and COO): "He accessed it from a personal device through a fully authenticated channel" and "The file and all surrounding prompts were deleted immediately upon confirmation of upload."

SEC Filing Details:
- Filed as Form 8-K under Item 1.05 (material cybersecurity incident)
- Determined material due to "the volume and sensitive nature of the non-public information at issue"
- No operational disruption or financial impact anticipated
- Employee was "mocking up a presentation" when uploading data

Regulatory Response:
The bank communicated with "relevant banking and financial regulators" and is notifying affected customers per federal and state law; no specific regulator statements given.

## WHY IT MATTERS
- FIRST-OF-KIND: first SEC Form 8-K filed under Item 1.05 triggered by unauthorized employee AI use rather than an external cyberattack.
- Named, established, non-tech-native org (regional community bank).
- Blameless victims: customers whose names, SSNs and DOBs were uploaded to a third-party AI service.
- No attacker. The "breach" was one employee mocking up a presentation on a personal device.
- Citable aftermath: SEC 8-K filing (public record), regulator notification, customer breach notices, CEO/COO on the record.
