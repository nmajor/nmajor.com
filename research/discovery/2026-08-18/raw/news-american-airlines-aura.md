# American Airlines AURA — AI rebooking removes passengers from flights

- URL: https://viewfromthewing.com/american-airlines-ai-keeps-removing-passengers-from-flights-they-run-to-the-gate-but-their-seats-are-gone/
- Publication: View From The Wing (Gary Leff)
- Date published: 2026-08-11
- Date accessed: 2026-08-18

## Verbatim / extracted

AURA = "AUtomated ReAccommodation." Internal documentation: it "identifies passengers that are certain to misconnect and utilizes that available inventory for protecting other passengers who may need that space."

The system removes passengers from connecting flights based on predictions they will miss the connection, then reassigns their seats to other passengers. This occurs BEFORE the passenger actually fails to make their flight.

Documented incidents at hubs: Chicago (ORD), Charlotte (CLT), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW), Mexico City (MEX).

Named affected passengers: Marc Thiessen (former Bush speechwriter, Washington Post columnist); Beverly Hallberg (media consultant); multiple other complainants referenced via Twitter links.
