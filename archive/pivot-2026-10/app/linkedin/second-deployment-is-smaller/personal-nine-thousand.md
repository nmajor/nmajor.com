Johnson & Johnson cut 85-90% of their AI implementations and initiatives. 

They found that 10 to 15% of their [nearly 9,000 AI use cases](https://www.tbpndigest.com/story/2025-04-18/johnson-johnson-cuts-9000-ai-projects-down-to-its-highest-value-use-cases-after-a-year-of-experimentation) were producing about 80% of the value.

They kept AI in 4 areas:

- a co-pilot for sales reps
- drug discovery
- supply chain risk
- an internal HR chatbot handling around 10 million employee interactions a year.

Its interesting to see where companies land with their AI implementations. AI initiatives are easy to get wrong. You need to get AI in places where it is suited to actually create value.
