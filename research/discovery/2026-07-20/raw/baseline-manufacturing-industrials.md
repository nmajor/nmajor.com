## Siemens raises 2026 outlook, driven by AI and data center demand — Manufacturing Dive
URL: https://www.manufacturingdive.com/news/siemens-raises-2026-outlook-ai-data-center-demand-q1/812182
(also syndicated: https://finance.yahoo.com/news/siemens-raises-2026-outlook-driven-111800365.html)

Dive Brief:
- Siemens AG raised its 2026 outlook following a first quarter that beat expectations, powered by the AI and data center boom in the United States.
- "We now expect to reach the upper half of our revenue growth guidance of 6% to 8%," CFO Ralf Thomas said. Raised EPS guidance by 20 euro cents.
- Orders increased 10% across its three divisions to 21.4 billion euros ($21.5 billion), led by record-high growth in smart infrastructure. **U.S. orders surged 54%** over last year due to strong data center and building software demand.
- Quarterly revenue grew 4% to 19.1 billion euros ($22.7 billion). Net income 2.2 billion euros ($2.6 billion) — roughly half of the year-ago period, which included a 3.5 billion euro gain from the Innomotics sale.
- Digital industries (industrial automation/technology) achieved double-digit growth in orders and revenue despite market softness.

Dive Insight: Customers such as Boeing, General Motors, Microsoft and Apple rely on Siemens' software and technology. Siemens is partnering with Nvidia on AI-driven manufacturing using digital twin and simulation technologies.
Roland Busch: "We are building the industrial AI operating system throughout the entire value chain, from design and engineering to manufacturing operations and into supply chain."
