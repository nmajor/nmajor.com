Who wrote the AI case study you're using to decide?

I went looking for one company that built its own tool with AI and published what it cost a year later. Not the launch post. The bill.

I couldn't find one. The most-quoted cautionary tale in the whole category turned out to be published on a site where every byline belongs to staff at a low-code and AI development agency.

Then I checked the success stories, including two I'd used myself. The numbers move between tellings, and none of them has a figure dated after year one.

Full piece in Actual Intelligence, my newsletter on plain-English AI for people who run traditional, non-tech businesses:
https://nmajor.com/writing/the-case-study-was-an-ad/

If you've built something in-house, what did year two actually cost you?
