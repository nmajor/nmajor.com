Boost is Better Than Ever: Turn Your Best Content into Business Growth

Authored byJonathan L.

Ads Platform @ LinkedIn

Great marketing starts with content that resonates. When a post sparks conversation, attracts new connections, or drives real engagement, that's signal — proof that your voice cuts through and your ideas land. Boost is built to take that signal and make sure it reaches the people who need to hear it most.

LinkedIn Boost amplifies the organic content you've already created, putting your best ideas in front of the audiences that matter most for your business. No new content to create. No ad expertise required. Just your strongest work, working harder.

For small business owners, marketers, and creators, that's a meaningful advantage. Boost is quick to set up, easy to manage alongside everything else you're running, and built for businesses that want results without complexity. It's why nearly 70% of new LinkedIn advertisers start here — and keep coming back.1

What Sets LinkedIn Boost Apart

LinkedIn's advantage is reach that's built on trust. With 130M+ decision-makers and 14M+ C-suite executives, it’s the most powerful platform to influence B2B decision-makers in the US.

It means your content doesn't just get seen — it gets seen by the right people. Our professional data ensures your ads land with verified business professionals, so every dollar of ad spend delivers.

Boost takes it further. It keeps your content authentic while turning it into a paid ad, so it resonates when it reaches the right professionals. That authenticity sparks genuine conversations and meaningful connections — especially valuable for small businesses building credibility in their industry.

New Features to Make Boosting Easier and More Effective

LinkedIn has improved Boost to better meet the needs of small businesses, creators, and founders. Here’s what’s new:

Boost your personal posts: Amplify Your Personal Brand

Previously, Boosting was only available for LinkedIn Pages. Soon, we're extending this feature to all members, allowing you to boost your personal posts.

Some of the best LinkedIn posts come from our members — experts, thought leaders, executives and others who drive trust, visibility, and growth on LinkedIn. 

Organic posting already delivers significant benefits; founders who post at least 10 times a year generate 33% more leads than those who don't.

Boosting your personal posts allows brands and creators to amplify authentic thought leadership and personal brand momentum without losing credibility. The results speak for themselves:

  • Members using Boost grow followers up to 2× faster 
  • Member-boosted posts achieve up to 1.8× higher click-through rates (CTR) than regular sponsored content.

For members that need to build authority and showcase their expertise, these numbers make a measurable impact.

Boost from your phone: Market Anytime, Anywhere

Marketing should fit into your schedule, not the other way around. We’ve started testing the ability to boost personal posts directly from your phone.In the coming months, all LinkedIn members will be able to boost both page and personal posts on mobile. Whether you’re commuting, traveling, or between meetings, you can act on opportunities as they arise, in just a few taps.

Boost your event : Maximize Event Impact

Events are a powerful way to showcase your business and build connections. By boosting an event on LinkedIn, you can create ads to promote your event and extend your event’s reach beyond your existing audience —without using complex  tools. On average, events promoted with ads see 31x more viewers and 4.4x more registrations. Drive registrations, increase live engagement, and extend post-event impact on LinkedIn—all within the event organizer experience itself. Learn more.

An updated Interface: Boosting made smoother 

We’ve simplified the boosting process with user interface enhancements designed for lean teams. When you’re juggling everything, the little things matter. Now, you can boost a post directly from its details view, cutting out extra steps and keeping you in your workflow. You'll also get notifications when a boost is approved, rejected, or about to end.

Bottom Line - Boosting Delivers Real Results

Here’s what small businesses and creators are achieving with Boost:

Member Boost Results:

  • 3–6× more reach, depending on campaign objectives, with top campaigns reaching 8–12× more.
  • Up to 1.8× higher CTR for member-boosted posts compared to regular sponsored content.
  • 2× faster follower growth for members using Boost, especially in early stages.

Page Boost Results:

  • Pages using Boost grow followers up to 2× faster than those that don’t.
  • Advertisers using page Boost typically see 2× more reach than those who don’t use page Boost, depending on their campaign objective with top campaigns achieving 3× more.

These improvements translate into tangible business outcomes, including increased website traffic, product awareness, higher engagement, and more opportunities for growth.

Make Boosting a Habit

To maximize Boost, think of it as a regular part of your marketing routine:

  1. Start with your best content. Look for posts already generating strong engagement—they’re worth amplifying.
  2. Set clear goals. Define whether you want to drive traffic, grow awareness, or increase followers, and align your boosted content accordingly.
  3. Use analytics. Monitor Boost performance to see what works and refine your approach for future campaigns.
  4. Experiment with content. Boost a mix of thought leadership, product updates, and event promotions to engage different audience segments.
  5. Leverage AI suggestions. Let LinkedIn’s AI optimize your audience targeting for the best results.

Make Your Content Work Harder

Great content opens doors, but only if it reaches the right people. Boost makes it easy to amplify your best work without adding complexity or straining your budget. With new features like Member Boost, Mobile Boost, and Event Boost, you can extend your content’s impact and drive meaningful results.

For small businesses, founders, and creators balancing multiple priorities, Boost is a fast, flexible, and effective way to grow. Ready to see what Boost can do for your business? Start amplifying your best content today and turn visibility into opportunity.
