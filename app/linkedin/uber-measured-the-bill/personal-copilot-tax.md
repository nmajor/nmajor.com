On July 1 Microsoft raised 365 prices by up to 43% and folded Copilot Chat into every base plan. There's no opt-out.

Business Standard went from $12.50 to $14 a seat. Frontline plans took the sharpest jump, up 33%, and 43% on the no-Teams variant. Existing customers keep their old price until renewal, so this arrives invoice by invoice through the year rather than all at once.

Run the math on a 200-seat Business Standard shop and it's about $3,600 a year of new spend. Worth keeping straight: the bundled thing is Copilot Chat, not the $30-per-seat full Copilot, which stays a separate add-on.

A metered tool can be capped, and a seat add-on can be dropped. This new per seat increase, you just have to pay whether you want it or not. But is it adding value enough to justify the price bump?
