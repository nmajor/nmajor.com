Domtar already paid for an analyst to help with its predictive-maintenance system. It was barely using the support.

After a motor failed, reliability engineer Matthew McLaughlin faced data from 450 sensors. He estimated that reviewing one day's output himself would take 32 weeks.

Domtar began using the vendor's included analyst, moved monthly calls to weekly and added readings from thermal, infrared and ultrasonic equipment. The team also tracked every equipment action and its response time.

McLaughlin says performance improved within three months and estimates about 1,547 hours of unplanned downtime have since been avoided. The company did not disclose the period behind that estimate or its AI cost.

The missing component was already in the contract. Someone had to put it into the operating routine.
