Rockwell interviewed technicians with 20 to 30 years on the factory floor before it built its maintenance assistant.

The technicians knew fixes that were missing from hundreds of pages of manuals. Rockwell's team documented that experience by symptom, cause and response, then combined it with maintenance history and data from the factory.

Azure AI Search retrieves the likely source material. The language model interprets the question and turns the retrieved material into a conversational answer beside the machine.

Rockwell reports that average repair time fell from 18 minutes to 12. The company has not published the measurement method, so that remains a reported result.

The LLM made the collection easier to query. It did not conduct the interviews, settle conflicting accounts or organize 30 years of experience.
