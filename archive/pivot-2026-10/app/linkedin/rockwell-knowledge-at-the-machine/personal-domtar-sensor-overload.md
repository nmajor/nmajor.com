Domtar had 450 AI-assisted sensors. A motor still failed.

The paper mill had collected so much vibration data that reliability engineer Matthew McLaughlin estimated one day's output would take him 32 weeks to analyze.

The fix was operational. Domtar began using the analyst support already included with the system, moved its vendor calls from monthly to weekly, added thermal, infrared and ultrasonic data, and tracked every action item.

Within three months, McLaughlin said performance improved. The mill now monitors 748 sensors, and he estimates the system has avoided about 1,547 hours of unplanned downtime.

That estimate has no published measurement period, and Domtar did not disclose cost. Installing the sensors created more data. Assigning people and a routine to act on it created the maintenance system.
