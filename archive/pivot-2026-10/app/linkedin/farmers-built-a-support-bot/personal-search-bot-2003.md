The support-search playbook was sitting in Computerworld in 2003.

Computerworld reported that LPL gave 4,300 representatives across 2,100 branches a natural-language search box over its internal portal. It wanted to move questions away from its service center and help staff avoid calls to supervisors.

The search engine returned relevant results 60% of the time out of the box. After several months of adding company terms and tuning results, LPL reported 80% relevance. Staff also reviewed incoming questions and added missing material to the portal.
