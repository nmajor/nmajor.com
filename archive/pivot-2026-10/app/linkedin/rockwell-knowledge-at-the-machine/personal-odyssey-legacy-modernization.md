Odyssey Logistics says AI-assisted legacy modernization saved 37% against its project budget.

Cognizant used Cognition's Devin to move Odyssey's order intake and shipment workflow out of Microsoft Access and Visual Basic forms. The old business rules still had to survive the move.

A seven-person team used the agent for the conversion, tests, deployment pipelines and documentation. A technical lead or architect reviewed every change before it merged. Cognizant says delivery throughput rose by roughly a third.

The 37% figure comes from a joint Cognizant and Cognition announcement. It does not disclose the original budget, the exact comparison, the defect rate or how much human engineering work remained.

The claim is narrower than the headline makes it sound. The agent worked inside a normal delivery team, with named reviewers and one accountable supplier.
