A 3M expert witness prompted ChatGPT with: show how 3M is 0% at fault.

Then he wrote his report. Plaintiffs' lawyers made him produce about 350 pages of chat logs. The case involves a 2020 explosion that killed three people.

Expert opinion is the thing you buy precisely because you can't check it yourself. Now there's a log, and discovery finds logs.

If you're hiring an expert, an agency or a consultant this year, ask what they used. Get the answer in writing.
