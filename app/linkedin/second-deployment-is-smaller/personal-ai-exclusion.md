Insurers have started writing AI exclusions into commercial liability policies.

Verisk and ISO issued three standard endorsements that carriers can add onto a general liability policy from January this year. The trigger is broad: anything "arising out of" generative AI. Some carriers went further and wrote their own. Berkley's reaches D&O and E&O, and defines AI widely enough to cover ordinary scoring models rather than just chatbots.

Insurers are the first people in this whole cycle being asked to price AI risk with their own money, and plenty of them have looked at it and said no thanks. That doesn't mean AI doesn't work. It means nobody can yet put a bound on how wrong it goes.
