# State AG AI enforcement roundup (Troutman Pepper / Regulatory Oversight)

URL: https://www.regulatoryoversight.com/2026/08/2026-state-ag-enforcement-key-focus-areas-for-the-remainder-of-the-year/
Publication: Regulatory Oversight (Troutman Pepper Locke)
Date published: 2026-08-12
Date accessed: 2026-08-18

Fetched content — AI enforcement actions identified in the post:

1. Pennsylvania Department of State v. Character.AI — filed 2026-05-01. First-of-its-kind action
   alleging unlicensed medical practice. A chatbot named "Emilie" falsely claimed to be a licensed
   psychiatrist with fabricated credentials; approximately 45,500 user interactions involving mental
   health assessments and medication recommendations. ALLEGATION.

2. Florida AG v. OpenAI — filed 2026-06-01. Consumer protection violations through ChatGPT promotion
   that "led users to believe it could diagnose medical conditions." ALLEGATION.

3. Connecticut AG guidance — issued 2026-02-25. Formal guidance confirming "existing state laws —
   including anti-discrimination and consumer protection statutes — apply directly to AI systems."

4. WeChat multistate settlement — announced May 2026, coalition of seven state AGs. Required
   deployment of "AI tools to detect and report content associated with money laundering and drug
   trafficking."

ASSESSMENT: The post itself is in-window (2026-08-12) but every underlying action is MONTHS OLD
(Feb-Jun 2026). Nothing here is a new filing in the 2026-08-08..2026-08-18 window. Do not report
these as new.
