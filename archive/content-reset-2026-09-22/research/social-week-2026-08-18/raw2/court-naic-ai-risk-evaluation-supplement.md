# NAIC Summer National Meeting — AI Risk Evaluation Supplement (regulator activity, in-window)

Date accessed: 2026-08-18
Status: PARTIALLY VERIFIED — sourced from web search summary of NAIC/law-firm materials, primary
NAIC page not opened directly. Treat as a lead, not a confirmed report.

Claim: On 2026-08-13, at its Summer National Meeting, the NAIC gave an update on its "AI Risk
Evaluation Supplement" — a structured set of inquiries to help state regulators examine how insurers
use and oversee AI. Renamed from "AI Systems Evaluation Tool" to reduce confusion about purpose.

"The supplement gives regulators a common way to gather evidence about AI use during market conduct
reviews, financial examinations, financial analysis or stand-alone inquiries."

Multistate pilot of an AI Evaluation Tool running January 2026 - September 2026, twelve states:
Colorado, Maryland, Louisiana, Virginia, Connecticut, Pennsylvania, Wisconsin, Florida, Rhode Island,
Iowa, Vermont, California. Anticipated adoption at the 2026 Fall National Meeting.
